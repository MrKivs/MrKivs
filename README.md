## 👋 About Me

Hey, I'm Lewis — a software developer, co-founder and CTO of **Byte Nuru**, a startup in
Nairobi building ERPs, portals and workflow systems for organizations that have outgrown
spreadsheets.

I like the unglamorous parts of building software as much as the launch: shaping the data model,
arguing about edge cases, designing the interface, then watching someone use it without asking
for help. My work usually sits where frontend, backend and payments meet — React and TypeScript
up front, Java, Spring Boot, Node and PostgreSQL behind it, and M-Pesa when a product needs to
move real money.

I'm also studying software development at **KCA University**. Outside of work I'm usually deep
in a side project, a design rabbit hole, or explaining why the demo is *almost* ready.

- 🤝 **Co-founder & CTO** at [Byte Nuru](https://www.bytenuru.com)
- 🏗️ **Currently building** Heron — crowdfunding, without the custody
- 🎓 **Studying** software development at KCA University
- 🎬 **Off the clock** on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MrKivs)
- 📫 **Reach me** at [kivululewis@gmail.com](mailto:kivululewis@gmail.com)

### Heron — Zero-Custody Crowdfunding
A platform for community fundraising: organizers publish campaigns, donors pay directly by
mobile money, and every verified payment is recorded without the platform ever holding or
routing the funds.

`React` `TypeScript` `Spring Boot` `PostgreSQL` · **Private build**
